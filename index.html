About

I've spent a little over a decade on the unglamorous half of software: consistency guarantees, encryption boundaries, control planes, and the APIs other teams have to live with for years. The work I'm proudest of tends to be invisible when it's going well.

I'm a principal engineer at Land id, working on auth, APIs, and infrastructure. Before that I spent four years at HashiCorp — leading discovery for an enterprise AI platform, and before that Project Infragraph, a real-time knowledge graph unifying resources, ownership, and policy across hybrid cloud. I led that team through the acquisition by IBM.

Alongside all of that I ran Music League for nine years as its sole engineer and operator, from zero to hundreds of thousands of users across six continents. That's where I learned what on-call really costs, and why I care so much about the boring parts.
